# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free learning and contribution
environment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background,
ability, education, or access to compute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and learning design—not people.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain assumed context.
- Credit sources and collaborators accurately.
- Respect privacy, consent, licenses, and responsible-use boundaries.
- Accept correction and help repair harm when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, plagiarism, credential exposure, and encouragement of unsafe or
illegal use are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement and scope

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's private
contact mechanisms. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public
issue. Maintainers may remove material or restrict participation when this
policy is violated. Reports will be handled as promptly and confidentially as
practical, without retaliation for good-faith reporting.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.
