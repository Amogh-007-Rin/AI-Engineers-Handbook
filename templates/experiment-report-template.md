# Experiment report

## Question and hypothesis

State one falsifiable question, expected outcome, and why it matters.

## Protocol

Record data/model/code versions, controls, seeds, metrics, compute, stopping rules, and deviations before results.

## Results

Report raw summaries, uncertainty, failures, and negative findings. Separate observation from interpretation.

## Decision and follow-up

State whether evidence supports the hypothesis, limitations, operational relevance, and the next discriminating experiment.
