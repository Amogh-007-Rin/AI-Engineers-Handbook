# Production AI engineering capstone

Propose a consequential but bounded AI system with a real or realistically represented stakeholder. Approval requires decision context, non-ML baseline, success/failure criteria, dataset feasibility, compute budget, threat model, ethical boundaries, and 8–12 week delivery plan.

## Required artifacts

- Requirements, architecture decision records, issue plan, and weekly evidence log.
- Versioned data pipeline, dataset card, validation, leakage review, and provenance.
- Baselines, experiments, uncertainty, error/slice analysis, and model card.
- Tested application/service with CI, pinned environments, deployment, observability, load evidence, SLO, cost, security controls, and rollback.
- Human oversight, accessibility, privacy, fairness, misuse, incident, and retirement plans.
- Demo, runbook, technical report, retrospective, and reproducibility package.

## Review gates

Proposal, data/baseline, model/evaluation, production/risk, and final defense are separate gates. Final scoring assigns 15 points each to product/framing, data, modeling/evaluation, engineering, and operations; 15 to responsible/security practice; and 10 to communication/reproducibility. Passing requires 80/100, no category below 70%, successful clean reproduction, a live failure-and-recovery drill, and resolution of every critical review finding.

A visually polished demo cannot compensate for leakage, fabricated evidence, unauthorized data, unbounded side effects, absent rollback, or results that cannot be reproduced.
