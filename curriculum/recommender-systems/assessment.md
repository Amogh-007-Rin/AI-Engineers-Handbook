# Recommender systems stage assessment

Build an offline retrieval/ranking evaluation. Pass at 80/100: 20 temporal/user
split integrity, 20 popularity and non-personalized baselines, 25 ranking plus
coverage/diversity metrics, 20 cold-start/feedback/privacy controls, and 15
latency/operations. Exposure leakage, test tuning, or no safe fallback
automatically fails. Remediation requires corrected event evidence and rerun.
