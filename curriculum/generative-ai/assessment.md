# Generative AI stage assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 corpus/retrieval integrity, 25 answer/grounding/citation
metrics, 20 adversarial safety and abstention, 20 reproducibility/latency/cost,
and 15 privacy/licensing/operations. Fabricated citations, unbounded context,
hidden live data, or no baseline automatically fail. Remediation requires a
fixed evaluation case and regression evidence.
