# Computer vision stage assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 image/annotation contract, 20 split and baseline integrity,
25 task metrics and visual error taxonomy, 20 robustness/performance/export,
and 15 privacy/bias/licensing/operations. Near-frame leakage, unlicensed assets,
or unchecked coordinate transforms automatically fail. Remediation requires a
data audit and regression evidence.
