# Reinforcement learning stage assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 environment/reward validity, 20 baselines and algorithm
rationale, 25 multi-seed evaluation, 20 robustness/safety/checkpoint evidence,
and 15 compute/reproducibility. Reward leakage, confused termination/truncation,
or single-seed claims automatically fail. Remediation requires corrected traces
and held-out evaluation.
