# Time-series stage assessment

Produce a rolling-origin forecasting report. Pass at 80/100: 20 timestamp and
availability contract, 25 leakage-safe origins and features, 20 naive/seasonal
baselines, 20 interval and regime error evidence, and 15 freshness/fallback/
retraining operations. Random splitting or future-derived features automatically
fail. Remediation requires corrected cutoffs and a full backtest rerun.
