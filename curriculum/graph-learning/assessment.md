# Graph learning stage assessment

Build and evaluate a graph prediction system. Pass at 80/100: 20 graph/schema
integrity, 25 message-passing and baseline reasoning, 25 entity/component/time
split validity, 15 degree/cold-start/robustness slices, and 15 persistence,
memory, and latency evidence. Structural or future-edge leakage automatically
fails. Remediation requires a corrected split, feature-only baseline, and rerun.
