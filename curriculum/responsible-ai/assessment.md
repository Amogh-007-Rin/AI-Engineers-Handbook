# Responsible AI stage assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 stakeholder/harm analysis, 20 privacy/security threat model,
20 fairness/accessibility evaluation, 20 mitigations/owners/monitoring/redress,
and 20 governance/release evidence. Missing affected groups, ownerless critical
risk, no appeal, secret exposure, or unsupported fairness claims automatically
fail. Remediation requires stakeholder-informed controls and tested rollback.
