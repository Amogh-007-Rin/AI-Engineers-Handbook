# Solution guidance

Start from stakeholders and plausible harms rather than model metrics. Link each
risk to affected groups, severity/likelihood evidence, preventive and detective
controls, an accountable owner, monitor, appeal/redress, and rollback. Critical
unaccepted residual risk blocks release. A complete submission also includes a
data-protection impact assessment, threat model, misuse cases, accessibility,
fairness uncertainty, incident procedure, and scheduled reassessment.
