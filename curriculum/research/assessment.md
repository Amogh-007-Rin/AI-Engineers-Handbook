# Research practice stage assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 source/claim and method fidelity, 20 baseline/data/environment
provenance, 25 statistical/deviation analysis, 20 extension/ablation evidence,
and 15 communication/ethics. Cherry-picked seeds, undisclosed deviations,
fabricated results, or missing licenses automatically fail. Remediation requires
a corrected experiment ledger and uncertainty-aware rerun.
