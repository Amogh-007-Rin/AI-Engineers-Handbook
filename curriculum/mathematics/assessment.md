# Mathematics stage assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 notation/shape fluency, 20 hand derivation, 25 executable
numerical verification, 20 probability/statistical interpretation, and 15
stability/limitations communication. Dimensionally invalid work, an unverified
derivative, or exact-equality claims for floating point automatically fail.
Remediation requires a hand-worked example, tolerance experiment, and corrected
regression tests.
