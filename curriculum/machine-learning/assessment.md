# Classical machine-learning stage assessment

## Part A — Reasoning

For a delayed-payment prediction system, define prediction unit/time, target, feature cutoff, action, error costs, baseline, split, metrics, and three deployment slices. Identify five leakage routes and explain how each is tested.

## Part B — Debugging

Review a pipeline that imputes and scales before splitting, tunes on the test set, joins a one-to-many table without validation, and reports accuracy alone. Produce a corrected evaluation design and regression tests.

## Part C — Practical

Complete the [stage project](project/README.md), then defend one rejected model, one threshold decision, and one limitation in a ten-minute review.

## Pass standard and remediation

Score at least 80/100 overall and at least 70% in each part. Leakage or test-set misuse requires redoing the affected experiment. Weak metric selection requires a new cost table and threshold analysis. Unsupported interpretation claims require a controlled counterexample and rewritten limitation section.
