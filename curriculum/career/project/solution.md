# Solution guidance

Lead with the problem, decision, and measured outcome; link every claim to a
specific test, metric, artifact, or review. Show baselines, rejected alternatives,
failures, limitations, and what changed—not only polished screenshots. Keep
private data and secrets out of public artifacts. A strong capstone proposal is
small enough to finish, defines acceptance and stop criteria, budgets compute,
assigns risks, and includes monitoring, rollback, and retrospective evidence.
