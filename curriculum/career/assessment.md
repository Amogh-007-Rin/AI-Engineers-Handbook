# Career and capstone readiness assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 evidence-backed portfolio narrative, 20 technical depth and
reproducibility, 20 stakeholder/decision communication, 20 capstone scope/risk/
evaluation/operations, and 20 demo and review responses. Unsupported claims,
secret or private-data disclosure, non-runnable work, or omitted limitations
automatically fail. Remediation requires corrected evidence links, a clean-run
record, and a revised scoped proposal.
