# Data stage assessment

Pass at 80/100: 20 schema/identity/time contracts, 20 missingness/range/category
quality, 20 join and leakage safeguards, 20 reproducible report/dataset card,
and 20 privacy/license/ownership/monitoring. Silent coercion, undocumented data,
many-to-many multiplication, or split identity leakage automatically fail.
Remediation requires quarantined evidence, corrected lineage, and rerun checks.
